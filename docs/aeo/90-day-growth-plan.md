# 90-day SEO and AI search growth plan

## Days 1–14: make the source accessible and establish evidence

Engineering releases the SEO delivery prerequisites, product reference and six guides after CI, staging and target-server Caddy validation. Confirm HTML without JS, robots/sitemap MIME types, canonical redirects, missing/private statuses, PWA and registration flows. Submit the working sitemap to the verified Google property and set up business-owned Bing reporting. Inspect real pages and crawler logs with the operator; distinguish UA probes from authenticated bot origins. Keep attribution off until the existing privacy/consent and staging conversion checks pass.

Growth runs the first monthly visibility matrix and records genuine results, including unavailable AI surfaces. Establish indexed/eligible pages, organic impressions/clicks/CTR and attributable pass/club/business-intent counts. Engineering measures mobile LCP/CLS/INP with available lab and field tooling, recording missing data instead of making performance claims. Product owners check every guide against the current tracking and registration behavior.

## Days 15–30: improve the existing sources

Fix crawl/canonical errors and inaccurate descriptions first. Help real public clubs and event organizers complete descriptions, dates, venues, addresses, eligibility and registration information through existing product controls. Request authentic links to actual Jump club/event pages from those organizers' own sites when appropriate; no bulk directory spam, fake reviews, paid endorsements or fabricated case studies. Outreach is an owner/growth activity, not an automated message sent by this change.

Compare guide visits and conversions with real search questions. Improve unclear explanations and internal links without turning every paragraph into an advert. Retain the short business landing copy. Event-city pages remain deferred until authoritative event city data exists; club location pages still require sufficient real public inventory. Check indexed pages and reports weekly during the release period.

## Days 31–60: strengthen accuracy and external references

Run the matched monthly AI review again. Identify which pages are cited, which claims are wrong and which relevant questions lack a useful answer. Correct the public source and seek correction through an appropriate product feedback path only when justified; do not assume updating schema changes a model's answer immediately. Add new guides only for real unanswered questions or recurring support issues, reviewed by a product/organizer owner.

Invite willing organizers to publish factual accounts of events or clubs they actually operate. Obtain consent before using names, photos or results. Keep authoritative facts consistent across Jump and genuine organizer pages. Compare referral categories and confirmed conversions while reporting blended Google/Bing traffic and missing referrers honestly. Consider an llms.txt link-index trial only after standard delivery works and a maintenance owner is identified; do not treat it as the main growth lever.

## Days 61–90: evaluate and prioritize the next release

Repeat the visibility review at day 60/90 with matched modes, locale and prompts, recording model changes and unavailable surfaces. Compare indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, cited URLs, supported versus inaccurate claims, identifiable AI visits and server-confirmed conversions as separate measures. Use matched periods and event-calendar context; small samples cannot establish causality or a ranking gain.

Choose the next small technical/content change based on evidence: inaccurate product explanations, incomplete event pages, measured LCP/INP issues, authoritative city data, a real business inquiry endpoint or stronger genuine organizer references. Expand successful useful content rather than mass-producing keyword pages. Summarize what changed, what remains unknown, who owns the next action and when it will be checked.

## Accountability and success criteria

Engineering owns delivery, privacy boundaries and regression checks. Product/operations owns factual accuracy and current listing information. Growth owns the monthly matrix, external report exports and authentic partnerships. A successful first 90 days produces accessible accurate sources and a repeatable baseline; it does not promise a particular ranking, AI recommendation, citation count or revenue increase. Use the measurement checklist and CSV template alongside this plan.
